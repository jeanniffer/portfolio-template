My design journey began 11 years ago, in agencies. But the real breakthrough came when I stopped designing for agencies and started teaching. The human exchange, learning and vulnerability woke something up in me: design is only powerful when it's rooted in human experience.

That realization changed the direction of my career. Since then, I've intentionally worked in spaces where design serves something bigger than a brand: nonprofits, mission-driven organizations, and communities working toward a more equitable future. I was supporting the people courageous enough to challenge injustice and imagine better systems.

Over the years, collaborating with organizations like **digitalundivided, Rainforest Foundation, and Academia.edu** has reinforced that belief. Design is amplification. The people driving social change already have the courage, expertise, and vision. My role is to help those ideas reach farther, resonate more deeply, and connect with the people who need to hear them.

As a Venezuelan, this mission is deeply personal. I've witnessed what happens when institutions fail to protect people's rights, but I've also witnessed something more powerful: communities coming together to build alternatives, support one another, and create change without waiting for permission. That experience shaped the kind of designer I strive to be.

That's why Open Society Foundations resonates with me. Your work recognizes that lasting change comes from strengthening the people and communities already leading it. I would be honored to contribute my design skills to help amplify those efforts, making complex ideas more accessible, movements more visible, and stories more impactful.

**I would love the opportunity to discuss how thoughtful, human-centered visual design can strengthen the movements building the more just and equitable future we all deserve.**
